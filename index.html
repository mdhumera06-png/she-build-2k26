RULES

  1. Participation is restricted to women students.
  2. Each team must have 2–4 members.
  3. Each team must have one Team Leader.
  4. Round-1 idea submission must be in PDF format.
  5. PDF size must not exceed 10 MB.
  6. Top 15 teams will be selected.
  7. The final hackathon will run for 24 hours.
  8. Each team must produce a working prototype.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate? +
Women students from colleges are eligible to participate.
How many members can be in a team? +
Each team must have a minimum of 2 members and a maximum of 4 members. Every team must have a Team Leader.
What is the hackathon theme? +
The theme of SHE BUILD 2K26 is Open Innovation.
Where will the hackathon take place? +
The hackathon will be conducted physically at Sri Aditya Degree College for Womens, Bhimavaram.
How long is the hackathon? +
The main hackathon is a 24-hour physical event, from November 14, 2026 at 8:00 AM to November 15, 2026 at 8:00 AM.
What should participants bring? +
Each team should bring at least one working laptop.
What are the registration fees? +
Round 1 requires ₹50 per participant. Teams selected for Round 2 pay ₹250 per participant, with the Team Leader paying for the whole team.
How many teams will be selected for Round 2? +
The Top 15 teams will be selected for the 24-hour hackathon.
